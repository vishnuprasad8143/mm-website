Container transport in Kochi

Port, CFS and customer-location container movement, run from Willingdon Island since 1997.

Based in Kochi

Container movement, close to the terminals

MM Transports has its office on Willingdon Island, close to Kochi's container terminals, including the Vallarpadam terminal. For close to thirty years we have moved containers between the port, container freight stations and customer locations in Kochi and across Kerala.

Importers, exporters and logistics companies use us for the short, time-sensitive moves around the port as well as the long runs to Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. Chakiat Group and MIV Logistics Pvt Ltd are among the companies we serve.

What we move in Kochi

  • Port to CFS container shifting
  • Empty container movement
  • Import and export container transportation
  • Dry container stuffing and destuffing
  • Low bed trailers for heavy and oversized cargo
  • Genset-equipped reefer container transport

See all six on the services page.

Beyond Kochi

From the port, across South India

Containers leave Kochi for Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh, including Coimbatore, Bangalore, Chennai and Hyderabad.
