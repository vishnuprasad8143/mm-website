About MM Transports

From 2 trucks to 50+ — our journey of delivering goods safely and on time across South India.

Who We Are

Built on Hard Work, Driven by Trust

MM Transports was founded in 2012 in Kerala with just 2 trucks and a simple promise — deliver goods safely and on time, every single time. What started as a small operation has grown into a fleet of 50+ trucks serving businesses across South India.

We are not a tech company. We are not a logistics platform. We are a transport company that owns trucks and moves goods. Our drivers know the roads. Our mechanics keep the trucks running. And our operations team makes sure your cargo reaches on time.

Over the past decade, we have earned the trust of hundreds of businesses — manufacturers, traders, and factory owners who rely on us to keep their supply chains moving.
