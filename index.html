What we move

Container haulage, done properly

We do one thing and we have done it since 1997 — move containers by road, safely and on schedule, between the Kochi port area and destinations across South India.

Container Transport

Our core work. Enclosed container trucks that keep cargo dry, sealed and secure from the moment it is loaded until it is signed for.

Port Container Movement

Container shifting to and from Kochi Port. Being based on Willingdon Island means shorter runs, faster turnaround and fewer surprises on gate timings.

Factory & Industrial Goods

Raw materials in, finished goods out. Regular movement for manufacturers who need the same trucks turning up at the same time each week.

Interstate Long Distance

Kerala to Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. Drivers who have run these corridors for years and know where the delays actually happen.

Why it matters

Every truck on your job is ours. Every driver is on our payroll.

Most transport you book today is passed down a chain of brokers before a truck actually moves. We do not work that way — never have. When something needs sorting, you are talking to the people who own the vehicle.

Direct accountability

One call reaches the family running the company. No agent, no middle layer, no passing blame down the line.

Our own maintenance

Because we own the fleet, we control how it is maintained. A breakdown is our cost, so we make sure it does not happen.

Nearly 30 years of it

Founded in 1997 and still running. In this trade, staying in business that long is the credential that counts.

Two generations

Started by the father. Run day-to-day by the son.

Manoharan Nagarajan founded MM Transports in 1997 and built it the slow way — one truck, one client, one delivery at a time. Nearly thirty years later he still sets the standard the company works to.

Since 2024 his son, M Vishnu Prasad, has been running operations and leading the expansion of the fleet. Same yard, same phone number, same way of doing business — with someone in the office every day who has grown up around these trucks.
