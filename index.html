Signature Works

Projects That Prove Our Promise

Every shipment has a story. These are a few that show how we protect value and deliver with discipline.

2025 · Textile & Apparel

Monsoon-Proof Delivery to Tiruppur

A last-minute order needed 120 tonnes of finished garments from Kochi to Tiruppur during the height of the monsoon.

  • Coordinated real-time weather reroutes and pre-tarped trailers to keep goods dry.
  • Secured priority unload slots at both plants to meet the factory's export window.
  • Delivered in 36 hours with zero damage, keeping the export client on schedule.
Distance · 360 km Timeline · 36 hrs
2024 · Heavy Machinery

Plant Relocation to Bengaluru Industrial Park

Moving heavy presses and jigs from Kochi to a client in Bengaluru required precise escort planning.

  • Dedicated escort convoy and experienced haulers kept the 52-tonne pieces compliant with Karnataka permits.
  • Engineered loading ramps and hydraulic shoring cut crane time by 40%.
  • Delivered ahead of the plant inauguration, enabling the client to start production on schedule.
Load · 52 tonnes Permits · Multi-state
2023 · Pharma & FMCG

Cold Chain Runs for Vaccine Distribution

Chilled vaccine suites had to reach remote clinics across Kerala and coastal Karnataka within 24 hours.

  • Maintained +2 to +8°C with GPS telemetry and dual power trucks.
  • Mapped alternate ports of entry to avoid congestion during peak travel.
  • All 38 shipments arrived with continuous temperature logs and zero spoilage.
Shipments · 38 kits Temp range · +2 to +8°C
