2026 · Renewables

Wind Turbines to Ramanathapuram

Oversized rotor blades and towers needed a calm, well-escorted corridor from Kochi harbour to the windfarm site during an easterly storm.

  • Engineered outriggers and dual-haul lanes to safely carry 3-section towers over 450 km.
  • Nighttime convoy with police escort kept traffic disruptions below 12 minutes per village crossing.
  • Delivered parts within 48 hours so turbines could commission on schedule.
Distance · 450 km Oversize · 13 m blades
2025 · Textiles

Monsoon-Proof Tiruppur Run

A Mumbai buyer demanded zero-moisture export-ready garments from Kochi within three days of order confirmation.

  • Preemptively recoated trailers, used sealed containers, and monitored humidity for every pallet.
  • Our operations team rerouted through Palakkad to avoid flooded coastal links.
  • Goods reached Tiruppur factory in 36 hours, keeping the export schedule intact.
Timeline · 36 hrs Mode · Container + tarping
2024 · Heavy Industry

Plant Re-location to Bengaluru

Moving 52 tonnes of presses, fixtures, and molds required multi-state permits and a synchronized crane plan.

  • Dedicated escort convoy and night-time convoy minimized road closures and kept speed consistent.
  • In-house mechanics accompanied the load to perform checks en route.
  • Client up and running two days before the planned inauguration.
Load · 52 tonnes Permits · 3 states
2023 · Pharma

Cold Chain Vaccine Runs

38 chilled vaccine kits had to reach district hospitals across Kerala and coastal Karnataka without a single temperature breach.

  • GPS-monitored reefer trucks maintained +2 to +8°C with dual battery backups.
  • Alternate border crossings were mapped to avoid bottlenecks during festivals.
  • Each drop came with certification and logbook ready for the health department.
Shipments · 38 kits Temp · +2 to +8°C
