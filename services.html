Container transport, start to finish

One fleet, one vehicle type, five ways businesses use us. Everything below runs on trucks MM Transports owns and drivers MM Transports employs.

What we offer

Five kinds of work, one standard

We are container specialists. That focus is deliberate — it is why we can commit to a delivery window and keep it.

Container Transport

Our core service. Enclosed container trucks carrying packaged, palletised and containerised cargo, sealed at pickup and opened at delivery.

  • Weatherproof, sealed transport
  • Dedicated truck per consignment
  • Direct pickup to delivery, no reloading

Port Container Movement

Container shifting to and from Kochi Port. Our yard is on Willingdon Island, inside the port area, so positioning runs are short and gate timings are familiar ground.

  • Based inside the port area
  • Short turnaround on port runs
  • Import and export container movement

Factory & Industrial Goods

Raw materials inbound, finished goods outbound. Built for manufacturers who need the same trucks arriving at the same time each week.

  • Factory-to-factory and factory-to-warehouse
  • Fixed pickup schedules
  • Drivers familiar with plant procedures

Interstate Long Distance

Kerala out to Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. Corridors our drivers have run for years, with realistic transit times rather than optimistic ones.

  • Kochi to Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Bangalore
  • Chennai, Hyderabad and Vizag runs
  • Interstate permits and documentation handled

Regular Contract Movement

For businesses with predictable volumes — a truck or several reserved on a recurring schedule, at a rate agreed up front.

  • Weekly or monthly schedules
  • Rates fixed in advance
  • Same drivers on your route

How it works

Three steps, no paperwork maze

Booking a truck with us should take one phone call.

Tell us the job

Call or WhatsApp with the pickup point, the delivery point, what you are moving and when it needs to go. You get a price back the same working day.

We assign the truck

We confirm which vehicle is on your job, the pickup time, and the driver's details — before the truck leaves the yard.

It gets there

Your cargo is loaded, moved and signed for. If anything changes en route, you hear it from us rather than finding out at the other end.

The difference

No brokers. No layers. No surprises on the invoice.

Every service on this page is delivered on our own vehicles by our own drivers. That is the whole proposition, and it has been since 1997.
