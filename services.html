Container transport and logistics

Container transportation, handling, heavy haulage, coastal shipping and reefer transport, run out of Willingdon Island, Kochi.

What we offer

Six services, one team

Seamless and cost-effective solutions for export and import businesses, from port to warehouse to inland destination.

Container Transportation

Safe and timely movement of containers for import and export, from Kochi across Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh.

  • Import and export container movement
  • Direct pickup to delivery
  • Interstate runs to Coimbatore, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad

Port & CFS Container Shifting

Container shifting between the port, CFS and customer locations, plus empty container movement for shipping lines and logistics companies.

  • Port to CFS container shifting
  • Empty container movement
  • Based on Willingdon Island, close to Vallarpadam

Dry Container Stuffing & Destuffing

Efficient handling of goods for secure loading and unloading of dry containers.

  • Secure loading and unloading
  • Careful handling of cargo
  • Arranged along with the transport

Low Bed Trailer Services

Specialised transport for heavy and oversized cargo that will not go in a standard container.

  • Heavy cargo
  • Oversized cargo
  • Quoted per job

Coastal Shipping Logistics

Reliable shipping solutions from Kerala to Gujarat, with the road leg handled by us at the Kochi end.

  • Kerala to Gujarat coastal route
  • Pickup and delivery by road
  • Booked through one point of contact

Genset-Equipped Transport

Temperature-controlled transport options for reefer containers, carried on genset-equipped vehicles.

  • Reefer container transport
  • Genset-equipped vehicles
  • Ask us about availability for your dates

How it works

Booking, in three steps

Booking a truck with us should take one phone call.

Tell us the job

Call or WhatsApp with the pickup point, the delivery point, what you are moving and when it needs to go. You get a price back the same working day.

We assign the truck

We confirm which vehicle is on your job, the pickup time, and the driver's details — before the truck leaves.

It gets there

Your cargo is loaded, moved and signed for. If anything changes en route, you hear it from us rather than finding out at the other end.
